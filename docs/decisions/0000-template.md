## Context

What situation forced a decision? What constraints applied at the time?

## Decision

What was decided, stated in one or two sentences.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and the specific reason each was rejected. Be concrete — "it was
slower" is useless; "MJX contact cost scales with possible rather than active contacts" is
actionable.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it commits us to.

## Revisit if

The specific observable condition that would justify reopening this decision.
